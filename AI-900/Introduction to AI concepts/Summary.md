# Summary – Introduction to AI Concepts

## Learning Objectives
Understand the kinds of solutions AI can enable and key considerations for responsible AI practices.

---

## Introduction to AI
- Overview of core AI capabilities.
- Focus on applications and principles rather than deep technical or mathematical detail.

---

## Generative AI
**Key Points**
- Enables creation of new content (text, images, video, code, etc.).
- Based on large language models (LLMs) or small language models (SLMs).
- LLMs are powerful and general but more costly; SLMs are cheaper and domain-specific.

**Scenarios**
- Chatbots and AI assistants.
- Content creation.
- Automated translation.
- Summarizing and explaining documents.

---

## Computer Vision
**Key Points**
- Trained on large sets of labeled images.
- Tasks: image classification, object detection, semantic segmentation.
- Can be combined with language models for multi-modal AI.

**Scenarios**
- Auto-captioning/tag generation.
- Visual search.
- Retail stock monitoring and checkout.
- Security monitoring.
- Facial recognition.
- Robotics and self-driving vehicles.

---

## Speech
**Key Points**
- **Speech recognition**: speech-to-text.
- **Speech synthesis**: text-to-speech.
- Improvements in noise handling, interruptions, and expressiveness.

**Scenarios**
- Personal assistants.
- Automated transcription.
- Audio descriptions.
- Speech translation.

---

## Natural Language Processing (NLP)
**Key Points**
- Models trained for text analysis.
- Cheaper non-generative models can handle many tasks.
- Common tasks:
  - Entity extraction
  - Text classification
  - Sentiment analysis
  - Language detection

**Scenarios**
- Document/call transcript analysis.
- Social media/product review sentiment analysis.
- FAQ chatbots or simple dialog systems.

---

## Data and Insight Extraction
**Key Points**
- Often uses OCR for text detection.
- Advanced models can extract structured data from documents, audio, images, and video.

**Scenarios**
- Automated form/document processing.
- Large-scale digitization.
- Document indexing.
- Meeting transcript summarization.

---

## Responsible AI
**Principles**
- **Fairness**: Avoid bias in training/testing.
- **Reliability & Safety**: Mitigate probabilistic errors.
- **Privacy & Security**: Protect training data and model outputs.
- **Inclusiveness**: Avoid exclusion of user groups.
- **Transparency**: Explain how AI works and its limitations.
- **Accountability**: Apply governance frameworks.

**Examples**
- Fair admissions systems.
- Safe robotics with confidence thresholds.
- Privacy in facial recognition.
- Accessibility in speech chatbots.
- Transparent AI in loan approvals.
